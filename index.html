Founder & CEO · 2025–2026

Tryps

An AI travel agent that plans and books a group trip from inside your group chat.

$250Kpre-seed on a YC SAFE
12person team
100beta users

Concept · where Tryps was heading

Mockups of the product we were iterating toward: Tryps turns plain-English group chat into native iMessage polls, then locks dates, holds flights, splits costs and runs the trip day to day.

I recruited and led the team across engineering, product and go-to-market, and owned the roadmap, hiring, budget and weekly operating cadence. Investors and senior advisors included former CEOs and technology leaders from Expedia, Western Union, Cognizant and Disney.

I also ran supplier and distribution strategy across global distribution systems and booking providers, testing commission economics and partner revenue models.

The companion app · latest design, April 2026

Why it ended

I chose to wind Tryps down instead of raising a bigger round, because there was no path to revenue without immense scale. The lesson I carry: talk to customers before you build.
